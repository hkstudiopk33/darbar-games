Privacy Policy

Court Piece Darbar: Bhabhi · Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information Court Piece Darbar: Bhabhi ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact us at hkstudiopk33@gmail.com.

1. Playing without an account

You can play every casual game offline without an account. Your progress (coins, XP, achievements, settings, cosmetics and avatar) is stored only on your device.

2. Information you give us

  • Account: full name, display name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, country, optional gender and a password (stored only as a salted hash). We use this to run your account, verify it with a one-time code and keep tournaments fair (one account per person).
  • Profile photo (Premium): if you upload a picture, it is resized and used as your avatar.
  • Premium Rewards Club: when you redeem Reward Points we use your account email to send the shopping voucher code, and keep a record of the request.
  • Table chat and friends: quick chats, emojis, throws and friend requests you send to other players.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Gameplay: games played, results, streaks, tournament entries and rankings.
  • Device and connection: device type, app version and an approximate network identifier (IP address). In tournaments the network is used to keep players on the same network at different tables (anti-collusion).
  • Online play: when you use Multiplayer, friends or tournaments, the app signs in anonymously with Google Firebase and stores your display name, player ID, friend list, game moves and table chat on Google's servers to run online play.
  • Advertising: free players see ads from our advertising partner (for example Google AdMob), which may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or limit it in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push token so we can send tournament and friend alerts. You can turn categories off in the app or switch notifications off in your phone settings.

4. Payments

The optional Premium subscription is bought through Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We do not receive your card details. Coins can never be bought.

5. How we use information

  • to run the games, accounts, tournaments, friends and notifications;
  • to prevent cheating, collusion, multiple accounts and fraud;
  • to show ads to free players and to provide Premium;
  • to fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app (hosting, email/SMS code delivery, push notifications, advertising, payments by the app store) and only as needed for those services, or when required by law. Other players see your display name, avatar, level, crowns and in-game stats.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data while your account is open. Voucher request records are kept for our accounts. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time – see Delete your account. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is for players aged 13 and over. Tournaments need an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections to our servers are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to some uses. Contact hkstudiopk33@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes and show the new date at the top.
